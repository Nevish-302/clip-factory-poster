## ielts_worst_01 (28s)
Hook: YOU'RE SCORED ON YOUR WORST ANSWER
Take: Practise the question you dread, not the one you love.
End: Which topic do you dread?
Caption: A chef is only as good as their worst dish, not their best. Same with the IELTS speaking test: your memorized answers are not your real level, how you handle a question you did not expect is.

## ielts_words_04 (32s)
Hook: SIMPLE WORDS BEAT FANCY WORDS
Take: Learn the plain words for each topic and use them on purpose.
End: What topic needs simple words?
Caption: Examiners change the topic so you cannot paste a list of band 9 words into every answer. They are more impressed by a simple word that fits the topic than a complex one that does not help.
Hashtags: #IELTS #IELTSSpeaking #Vocabulary #LearnEnglish #EnglishSpeaking #Reels

## ieltsb_testmode_01 (40s)
Hook: BAND 9 TO BAND 5 IN 10 MINUTES
Take: Talk to the examiner like a friend, not a judge.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Before class, his students chatted at band 9 level. Once he said "let's do a mock test", they dropped to band 5 or 6. One question fixed it: why don't you speak like you did 10 minutes ago?
Hashtags: #IELTS #IELTSSpeaking #EnglishSpeaking #SpeakingPractice #LearnEnglish #Reels

## ieltsb_offtopic_02 (37s)
Hook: WHY EXAMINERS STOP YOU MID-ANSWER
Take: Answer first, add one detail, then stop.
End: Has an examiner cut you off?
Caption: If you cut in during an IELTS speaking test, it is not because the examiner is rude. They have 12 to 15 questions to get through in part one, so going off topic makes them stop you.
Hashtags: #IELTS #IELTSSpeaking #EnglishSpeaking #StudyAbroad #LearnEnglish #Reels

## ieltsb_coffee_03 (26s)
Hook: IMAGINE YOU'RE IN A COFFEE SHOP
Take: Picture a friend asking the question, then just answer.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Too many frameworks making you stiff? Try the coffee shop method: imagine a friend is asking you the question. You would not give a memorized answer or stuff in fancy vocabulary, you would just answer.
Hashtags: #IELTS #IELTSSpeaking #EnglishSpeaking #SpeakingTips #LearnEnglish #Reels

## ieltsb_memorize_04 (29s)
Hook: MEMORIZED ANSWERS CAN GET A ZERO
Take: Use your own simple words, not a script.
End: Do you memorize answers?
Caption: IELTS is not a memorization test. Examiners are trained to spot rehearsed answers, and the speaker says they can even get you a band zero. If you could really speak at that level, you would not need to memorize.

## nyema_blame_01 (38s)
Hook: STOP BLAMING OTHERS
Take: Notice when you hand your mood to someone else, then take it back.
End: Does this sound like you?
Caption: Two habits that keep happiness unstable, according to Gen Kelsang Nyema: crediting other people and events for your good mood, and blaming them for your bad one. Which one do you do more?

## nyema_balloon_02 (49s)
Hook: WHO RUNS YOUR MOOD?
Take: If one annoying person flips your mood, your mood is on loan.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: If a disliked colleague or one thing going wrong makes your good mood vanish, your mood is being pushed around like a balloon in the wind. Gen Kelsang Nyema on noticing that pattern.
Hashtags: #Happiness #Mindset #WorkLife #EmotionalAwareness #TEDx #Reels

## nyema_goodday_03 (44s)
Hook: WHO OWNS YOUR GOOD DAY?
Take: Next time you rate your day, check how much was in your control.
End: What made today good? Tell us
Caption: When someone asks "did you have a good day?", the reasons are usually a list of outside things. Gen Kelsang Nyema says that makes your happiness depend on people and circumstances you cannot control.
Hashtags: #Happiness #Mindset #GoodDay #SelfAwareness #TEDx #Reels

## nyema_response_04 (55s)
Hook: HOW YOU RESPOND MATTERS
Take: Pause before reacting: your response is the part you control.
End: How do you reset? Tell us
Caption: It is not what happens that makes you happy or unhappy, it is how you respond to it. Gen Kelsang Nyema says a calm state of mind keeps you steady, and an agitated one spoils even good circumstances.
Hashtags: #Happiness #Mindset #CalmMind #EmotionalHealth #TEDx #Reels

## aisha_choice_01 (32s)
Hook: HAPPINESS IS A CHOICE
Take: You can't control what happens, but you can pick your attitude.
End: Do you agree? Tell us
Caption: Aisha Chaudhary, 17 at the time of this talk, on why she chooses a happy attitude through difficult times: being miserable would not make things better, so she may as well choose to be happy.

## aisha_memories_02 (41s)
Hook: MAKE A GOOD MEMORY
Take: Plan one thing to look forward to after a rough day.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: After a long day of hospital appointments, Aisha Chaudhary and her brother went to a show in London. Her point: create many happy memories, so they can crowd out the sad ones.

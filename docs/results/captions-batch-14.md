## itachib_love_01 (41s)
Hook: SAVING SOMEONE ISN'T THE SAME AS LOVING THEM
Take: Protecting someone from danger but not from hurt is only half of love.
End: Who protected you from more than danger?

## naruto_shy_01 (28s)
Hook: SHY DOESN'T MEAN NO DREAMS OF YOUR OWN
Take: Quiet people still need a goal that is theirs.
End: Follow for a clip a day

## naruto_love_02 (33s)
Hook: LOVE ALONE ISN'T A GOAL OF YOUR OWN
Take: Love can drive you, but pick a goal that is also your own.
End: What goal is yours alone?
Caption: Love is never a bad motivation, the narrator says, but when nearly every woman in a story is driven by the same thing, it says nothing about independence or self-worth. A goal that is only yours matters.
Hashtags: #Anime #Naruto #Writing #Storytelling #Reels

## naruto_sakura_03 (34s)
Hook: A GOAL ABOUT SOMEONE ELSE ISN'T YOURS
Take: If your plans only revolve around one person, write one for you.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: The narrator's take on a main character: she does not need a dark backstory to have depth, but she needs goals that are not about someone else. Who are your goals really about?

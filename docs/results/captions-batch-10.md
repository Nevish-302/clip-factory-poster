## mattis_improve_01 (43s)
Hook: ASSUME YOU MUST KEEP IMPROVING
Take: Pick one habit and beat last week's number.
End: What will you improve this week?
Caption: Gen. Mattis says staying teachable starts with one decision: assume you must keep improving. Treat every week as your last week to prepare, accept no excuses, and be the one others can turn to.
Hashtags: #Leadership #SelfImprovement #Discipline #Growth #Mindset #Reels

## mattis_reading_02 (30s)
Hook: READ SO YOU'VE SEEN IT BEFORE
Take: Read how others handled your problem before it hits.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Gen. Mattis on how he stayed sharp under pressure: he had read so much that when trouble came he had already seen similar situations and knew how they were handled, well or badly.
Hashtags: #Leadership #Reading #Learning #Decisions #Mindset #Reels

## mattis_trust_03 (49s)
Hook: EARN TRUST, NOT POPULARITY
Take: Skip the favors; show up daily and be fair.
End: Who earned your trust?
Caption: Gen. Mattis on what separates great teams: you earn trust and respect every day. Not popularity with favoritism, but real affection strong enough that people stick by one another.
Hashtags: #Leadership #Teamwork #Trust #Management #Respect #Reels

## mattis_reason_04 (48s)
Hook: I HATED PARTS OF THE JOB
Take: Motivation comes from the people, not the tasks.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Gen. Mattis admits there were parts of the job he hated for his whole career. What kept him going was the people he served alongside, not the tasks.
Hashtags: #Leadership #Motivation #Work #Purpose #Mindset #Reels

## mattis_excuse_05 (37s)
Hook: DIFFICULTY IS NOT AN EXCUSE
Take: Bring a fix with the problem, not just the complaint.
End: Do you make excuses or plans?
Caption: What Gen. Mattis looked for in his team leaders: initiative and the drive to carry out the order. He was not interested in hearing that something was difficult, because difficulty is not an excuse.
Hashtags: #Leadership #Initiative #Accountability #Management #Discipline #Reels

## yc_terrible_01 (29s)
Hook: GREAT IDEAS LOOK LIKE BAD ONES
Take: Don't kill an idea just because friends laugh at it.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Sam Altman: the best startup ideas often look terrible at the start. Google was the 13th search engine, Facebook the 10th social network, Airbnb sounded like staying on strangers' couches.
Hashtags: #Startup #Entrepreneurship #SamAltman #YCombinator #Ideas #Reels

## yc_market_02 (40s)
Hook: CHANGE ANYTHING BUT THE MARKET
Take: Check the market is growing before you build.
End: Which market is growing for you?
Caption: Sam Altman: you cannot create a market that does not want to exist. You can change almost everything in a startup except the market, so look for one that is small today but growing fast.
Hashtags: #Startup #Entrepreneurship #SamAltman #YCombinator #Business #Reels

## yc_pager_03 (35s)
Hook: REPLY TO USERS WITHIN AN HOUR
Take: Set an alert so a user's 3am email never waits.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Sam Altman on fanatical founders: some YC founders wire their support inbox to an alert so a user who emails in the middle of the night still gets a reply within an hour.
Hashtags: #Startup #Founders #CustomerSupport #YCombinator #Entrepreneurship #Reels

## yc_pinterest_04 (27s)
Hook: GET USERS BY HAND, NOT ADS
Take: Find your first ten users face to face.
End: Where would you find your first 10?
Caption: Sam Altman: do not buy ads for your first users, recruit them by hand. Pinterest's Ben Silbermann signed up strangers in coffee shops and set browsers in an Apple store to Pinterest.
Hashtags: #Startup #Founders #Pinterest #YCombinator #Entrepreneurship #Reels

## yc_bad_05 (28s)
Hook: THEY CALL IT BAD? GOOD NEWS
Take: Weird idea, few rivals: ask why others aren't doing it.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Sam Altman: if you come up with a great idea, most people will think it is bad. That is good news, because it means they will not compete with you. Aim for sounds crazy, but actually right.
Hashtags: #Startup #Entrepreneurship #SamAltman #YCombinator #Ideas #Reels

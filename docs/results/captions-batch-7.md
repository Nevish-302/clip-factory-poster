## psych_name_01 (44s)
Hook: SAY THEIR NAME TO GET THEIR ATTENTION
Take: Use it early in the chat, not in every sentence.
End: Who always uses your name?
Caption: Hearing your own name pulls you back into the moment, which is why teachers use it. Psych2Go's tip: use someone's name at the start or end of a question to hold their attention.

## psych_askbig_02 (40s)
Hook: ASK BIG FIRST, THEN ASK SMALL
Take: Know what you really need before you open with the big number.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Ask for the bigger favor first and the smaller one after feels easier to agree to. Psych2Go calls it the door-in-the-face technique, and shows it with a 20 versus 50 loan.
Hashtags: #Psychology #Communication #DoorInTheFace #SocialSkills #Persuasion #Reels

## psych_mirror_03 (34s)
Hook: COPY THEIR GESTURES TO MAKE FRIENDS
Take: Gentle and natural wins. Do not turn it into a mime act.
End: Tried this to meet someone new?
Caption: New at school or work? Psych2Go suggests gently mirroring someone's movements and gestures: people tend to warm up to someone who feels similar. It is called the chameleon effect.

## psych_nod_04 (38s)
Hook: NOD WHILE YOU MAKE YOUR POINT
Take: Try it in your next meeting and see who nods back.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Want someone to hear you out? Psych2Go cites a 1980 study: when listeners nod, they are more likely to agree. Nod gently while you explain your point and see what happens.

## psych_gum_05 (32s)
Hook: STUDY AND TEST WITH THE SAME GUM
Take: Cheap to try: same flavour in practice and on exam day.
End: Would you try this?
Caption: Psych2Go's study idea: chew one distinct flavour of gum while you revise, then chew the same flavour in the exam so the taste acts as a memory cue. Cheap to test on your next quiz.

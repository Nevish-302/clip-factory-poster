## driver_fundamentals_01 (30s)
Hook: YOU DON'T NEED NEW TRICKS, JUST THE BASICS DONE WELL
Take: Skip the clever shortcut this week: do the plain fundamentals of your job better.

## driver_onepage_02 (30s)
Hook: PLAN THE WHOLE THING ON ONE SHEET OF PAPER
Take: Before a big project, put the whole plan on one page and see the gaps.
End: Do you plan on paper?
Caption: Edgar Wright on how he plotted Baby Driver: the whole act structure on one sheet of paper, methodical and mathematical. A simple habit for any big project.
Hashtags: #Planning #Storytelling #Filmmaking #Productivity #Reels

## driver_consistent_03 (35s)
Hook: SMALL KINDNESSES ADD UP TO WHO YOU ARE
Take: Be the same person at the start and the end: small kind acts are remembered.
End: Who stayed the same for you?
Caption: The film's hero is loyal to who he is from the first scene to the last, and his small acts of kindness are what count in the end. A storytelling lesson that works for real life too.
Hashtags: #Character #Kindness #Storytelling #Integrity #Reels

## driver_show_04 (30s)
Hook: YOU CAN SHOW WHO YOU ARE WITHOUT SAYING A WORD
Take: People read what you do, not what you say: let your actions introduce you.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Six minutes with almost no dialogue, and we still know who the hero is: what he avoids, what he enjoys, what he is good at. Everything is shown, nothing is announced.

## donna_contract_01 (42s)
Hook: SHE NEGOTIATED HER BIG BREAK INTO HER CONTRACT
Take: Ask for the opening you want before you need it: put it in the deal.
End: What would you ask for?
Caption: Donna Mills knew she wanted to leave New York, so she negotiated nighttime guest shots in L.A. into her soap opera contract. One of them led to her first movie role.

## donna_star_02 (35s)
Hook: WANT TO BE A STAR? DON'T. LOVE THE WORK.
Take: Pick work you would do unseen: fame alone is a roll of the dice.
End: Follow for a clip a day
Caption: Donna Mills' advice to young actors: if you love the work, go for it. If you only want to be a star, you will probably be disappointed.

## donna_producer_03 (45s)

## donna_redo_05 (28s)

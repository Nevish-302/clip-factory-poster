## 2026-10-27
- Problem reported: nothing was posted on 26 Oct. Cause 1 (bug): `post-due.mjs` posted only the first 2 due entries per run and two clips (`jackma_amazon_01`, `pichai_fire_01`) failed on Instagram on every run, so they used both slots and everything behind them (5 clips overdue) never posted. Cause 2 (platform change): since about 25 Oct Instagram's Reels API rejects clips above a resolution-times-length ceiling (`ProcessingFailedError` at the final upload check), including a clip that had posted fine on 25 Oct. Found with a new no-publish probe (`ig-probe.mjs`): 1080p 20 s ok / 30 s fail; 720p 53 and 59 s ok / 79 and 89 s fail; 540p 79 and 89 s ok; chunked uploads fail only on the last chunk; the account and the publishing quota were fine. Cause 3: GitHub's scheduled runs fire only about 4 to 6 times a day, so recovery is slow without a manual dispatch.
- Fixes: `post-due.mjs` parks a target after 3 failed attempts, posts up to 3 per run and reports parked entries in `--status`. New `ig-encode.sh` (picks 1080p, 720p or 540p by length) and `reencode-pending.sh` (re-encodes every unposted queue clip and replaces its video in the release). The 5 overdue clips were re-encoded, replaced and posted by dispatching the workflow twice; the Jack Ma clip needed a 720p encode. 12 of 60 clips posted so far. All remaining clips are being converted. Stdin bug found while doing it: ffmpeg consumed the `while read` input (needs `-nostdin`).
